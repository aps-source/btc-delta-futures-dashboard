Rules

    Candle data: Delta Exchange public history API (/v2/history/candles), BTCUSDT perpetual futures, 1d/4h/1h resolutions, UTC calendar days. No-repaint: Daily/4h/pivot/CPR levels always use the previous FULLY CLOSED period, held flat until the next period closes. P&L is measured in raw USD points on the underlying (long = long the future, short = short the future) — no funding-rate carry, fees, or slippage are modeled, so real returns will differ from this proxy. Single position at a time; a signal on the opposite side while a position is open is ignored until that position exits. This page fetches state.json from the same origin every 60s — refresh comes from a scheduled local tick (live_tick.py), not a live exchange connection to your browser.
